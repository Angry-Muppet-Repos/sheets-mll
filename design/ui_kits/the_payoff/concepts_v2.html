At setup you write letters to your future self — day-one you, talking to halfway you. They hide (script-hidden rows) and unseal automatically when you cross each rank. No app does this; a spreadsheet can. Plus grace tokens: two per year, so one brutal month dents the streak instead of killing it — broken streaks are where trackers get abandoned.

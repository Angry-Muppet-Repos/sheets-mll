2ONE WALL PER DEBT — BRICKS, NOT BARS

Auto loan · 15 of 34 bricks laid · $250 each
Each debt gets a little wall on Progress: a brick per $250 paid, the gold brick is the one your next payment lays. Paid-off debts show a finished wall with a seal. The per-debt version of the thermometer the category loves — minus the clip-art.

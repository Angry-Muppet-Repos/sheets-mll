Column & Co.

The Foundation v2.1

Setup in 5 steps

  1. Install the script — a 💳 Column & Co. menu appears.
  2. Add your accounts on the Accounts tab with current balances.
  3. Type the account name in C10 of Bank Import, paste your CSV, run Import.
  4. Set goals on the Goals tab — progress tracks automatically.
  5. Explore Dashboard, Trends, Health Score, and Net Worth.

Themes

Menu → Apply Theme → pick one of 16 palettes. The brand chrome stays locked; only your content retints.

Budget profiles

Menu → Apply Budget Profile → choose from 10 methodologies, or pick Custom and edit any yellow cell, then Save current values as Custom.

Ask an AI

A hidden _Schema tab documents your workbook for Claude or ChatGPT. Attach your sheet and ask away.
